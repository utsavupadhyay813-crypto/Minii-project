🗳️ Online Voting System

A transparent, secure, and verifiable digital balloting platform with strict voter privacy and ballot secrecy.

🔐 Secure & Verified

Voter registration requires admin identity verification before voting privileges are granted, safeguarding against illegitimate ballots.

🕵️ 100% Anonymous Ballots

Ballot secrecy is guaranteed by completely decoupling participation audit records (who voted) from ballot selections (what was chosen).

☝️ One Person, One Vote

Strict uniqueness constraints prevent duplicate submissions per election, ensuring total election integrity.

Quick Workflow Overview

1. Election Setup
Admins create elections, schedule start/end windows, and nominate 2+ candidates with custom symbols and manifestos.
2. Voter Verification
Voters register and receive a unique ID (e.g. VTR260000X). Admins review and verify eligibility.
3. Balloting & Auditing
Verified voters cast secret ballots. Real-time turnout analytics, bar charts, and official printable PDF reports are generated.
